Feature Guide

Click the Sliders icon next to the "Select" button to open the Advanced Filter panel. Here you can filter your lists by Movie/TV, Release Year (Decades), and Runtime Length.

Inside the Sort Panel, you can click FlowMode to hide titles you've recently viewed, creating an ultra-clean UI. You can also sort your lists by Date Added, Name, Rating, and Year.

When viewing the details of a TV show you are currently watching, use the Season + / - buttons to quickly mark an entire season as watched, jumping your episode count automatically.

If you decide to drop a show or movie but want to remember where you left off, click Drop in the detail modal. It will be moved to the hidden Archive tab (the box icon) where you can always restore it later.

Can't decide what to watch? Head over to the Explore tab and use the Random Pick feature to get smart, randomized suggestions from TMDB.

In the Data settings tab, you can back up your entire watchlist as a JSON file, or import your data directly from Letterboxd or IMDb CSV exports!
