Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What is FlowMode?
FlowMode is CineQ's intelligent algorithm that automatically organizes your watchlist. It balances factors like ratings, genres, and your watch progress to suggest the perfect viewing order, eliminating decision fatigue.
Can I import my data from Letterboxd or IMDb?
Yes! CineQ has a built-in import feature that allows you to upload your Letterboxd or IMDb history. It instantly moves your entire catalog over so you don't have to start from scratch. (Limit: 500 items per import, up to 3 imports daily).
How does the Stats Ticket work?
CineQ transforms your watch history into a beautifully designed digital cinema ticket. You can track your total watch time, movies watched, and instantly share your unique "Cinematic Identity" with your friends.
How do I track movies that aren't released yet?
If a movie or TV show hasn't been released, simply click the "Notify Me" bell icon. CineQ will add it to your reminders list so you won't forget when it finally drops.
Can I review movies I've already watched?
Absolutely. Our "Experience" feature allows you to rate watched content using a dynamic half-heart system and leave a brief comment to remember your thoughts.
Is CineQ completely free to use?
Yes, CineQ is completely free and ad-free. We built this platform out of pure passion for film enthusiasts to enjoy without paywalls.
