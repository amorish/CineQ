Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 25, 2026

Welcome to CineQ. Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our website (https://cine-q.vercel.app).

1. Information We Collect

We collect very limited information to provide you with CineQ's core watchlist synchronization features:

  • Google Profile Info: When you log in with Google, we receive your email address, name, and profile picture to identify you securely.
  • Account Info: For standard email signups, we store your chosen username and email address.
  • Watchlist Data: We save your movie and TV show watchlist, scores, status, and episode tracking progress.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to synchronize your personal movie and TV series watchlist, store your interface preferences, and provide you with a unified experience across devices.

3. Data Protection and Storage

Your data is stored securely in Google Cloud Firestore. We follow security best practices to protect your data from unauthorized access or disclosure. We do not sell, share, or disclose your personal data to any third-party services.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: mycineq@gmail.com.
